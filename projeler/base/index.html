To deliver fast, reliable, and sustainable mobility solutions that create competitive advantage for our customers in special vehicles and defense, powered by our advanced engineering capabilities.

Who We Are

Founded in 2016, BASE Studio Arge ve Teknoloji Sanayi A.Ş. is a trusted solution partner to leading heavy-duty vehicle OEMs, delivering complete drivetrain systems for 4×4, 6×6, and 8×8 configurations, as well as fully integrated, turnkey rolling chassis platforms offered to accelerate time-to-market.

BASE's proprietary systems — including axles, transfer cases, and thru drives — are engineered and manufactured in-house and, optionally, delivered as integrated rolling chassis platforms. All solutions are rigorously validated in the demanding combat conditions of Europe, Turkey, the Middle East, and Africa, ensuring best-in-class durability, reliability, and performance.

Guided by the principles of transparency, trust, and collaboration, BASE operates with a customer-first mindset. Through agile development and flexible production capabilities, the Company helps the specific needs of OEMs.

SUPPLIER OF STRATEGIC CONTENT

Powertrains are changing. The drivetrain is not. Axles, hub reduction and suspension carry across to electric and alternative-fuel platforms — so BASE content doesn't shrink with the engine, it becomes a larger share of what remains.

10 / LEAD GENERATION

The experience does not begin
with a registration form.

This is essential. Let visitors experience the installation first. Only request personal information when the visitor asks BASE to provide something valuable.

NEVER

Form first

A gate at the entrance costs engagement and produces low-quality data from visitors who never saw the product.

ALWAYS

Value first

The form appears only after SEND MY CONFIGURATION or TALK TO AN ENGINEER — when the visitor has already asked BASE for something.

SHORT

Four fields

Name · Company · Country · Business email. Optional project timeframe. Nothing else.

OPTIONAL

Scan event badge

Badge-scanner integration presented as an optional technical integration, subject to IDEX exhibitor technology / API availability.

Consent statement is a placeholder to be adapted to BASE's privacy requirements and applicable regulations (incl. KVKK / GDPR and UAE data provisions) during technical development.
