visitors see hundreds of products.

04 / THE EXPERIENCE

Five moves.
Under five seconds to understand.

  1. 01

    CHOOSE

    2, 3 or 4 axles. The visitor starts from the only decision that matters to them: how much platform their mission needs.

  2. 02

    CONFIGURE

    Environment and mission requirement. Optional engineering controls for payload, gradient, terrain severity and climate.

  3. 03

    EXPERIENCE

    The two LED walls become one environment around the configured platform. Engineering View strips the body away and reveals the BASE axle.

  4. 04

    CHALLENGE

    How far would you push it? The visitor raises payload, gradient, severity and climate — and watches the environment get harder.

  5. 05

    TAKE IT WITH YOU

    The configured platform takes over both walls. The visitor leaves with a personalised BASE Mobility Profile.

05 / THE INTERFACE

Working prototype.

Everything below is live. Use the touchscreen — the two LED walls respond exactly as they would on stand 12-E13.

LED WALL SIMULATION

Two surfaces, one panorama. The scene is a single continuous environment split across both walls; the corner is rendered as a curve, not a cut.

TOUCHSCREEN CONTROLLER

Large targets, minimal menu depth, engineering tool language. Designed for a landscape touchscreen at standing height.

VISUALISATION, NOT SIMULATION

Force, traction and articulation readouts are exhibition visualisations of engineering capability — not validated simulation output. Replaceable with BASE datasets.

06 / ENGINEERING VIEW

The body disappears.

10 / LEAD GENERATION

The experience does not begin
with a registration form.

This is essential. Let visitors experience the installation first. Only request personal information when the visitor asks BASE to provide something valuable.

NEVER

Form first

A gate at the entrance costs engagement and produces low-quality data from visitors who never saw the product.

ALWAYS

Value first

The form appears only after SEND MY CONFIGURATION or TALK TO AN ENGINEER — when the visitor has already asked BASE for something.

SHORT

Four fields

Name · Company · Country · Business email. Optional project timeframe. Nothing else.

OPTIONAL

Scan event badge

Badge-scanner integration presented as an optional technical integration, subject to IDEX exhibitor technology / API availability.

Consent statement is a placeholder to be adapted to BASE's privacy requirements and applicable regulations (incl. KVKK / GDPR and UAE data provisions) during technical development.

14 / LEAD INTENT METHODOLOGY

Four understandable stages.
